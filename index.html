How It Works

From the field to your Due Diligence Statement

1

Field Registration

Our team goes farm by farm: GPS boundaries, land tenure documents, photos, and production data, captured in person through the Hereda App.

2

Deforestation & Risk Audit

Every plot is cross-checked against satellite data to confirm it's deforestation-free, and assessed against EUDR risk criteria.

3

Farmer Digital Identity

Each farmer gets a verified, ongoing digital record, not a one-time data entry, but an identity they hold and build on.

4

Exporter Dashboard & DDS

You get full visibility into your verified supply chain, plus the documentation to generate and submit your Due Diligence Statement.
